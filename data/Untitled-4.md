## Martha Ministry

Currently, when planning a funeral at St. Joseph's with a member of staff, the bereaved family is offered the opportunity to hold a reception at St. Joseph's following the funeral Mass. It can be as simple as pastries and coffee (with donated, home-baked treats and coffee/tea/juice) or a catered luncheon that the family has ordered. Volunteers set up the reception, host the family/take care of their needs while they are our guests and then clean up the room following their departure.

There are several ways you can serve this ministry! You can either be a

<!-- image -->

baker, or you can help out as a host at the reception. Also, if the family has pre-ordered food, you can pick it up and then deliver it to St. Joseph's.

" I find that sharing hospitality with our fellow parishioners is a wonderful way to help strengthen my own faith while building community within our parish."~ A Martha Ministry Disciple

<!-- image -->

## Outreach Calls

The Outreach Call Ministry continues beyond the time of the funeral. Guided volunteers assist with follow-up calls, done in total confidentiality, approximately one month beyond the date of death. This ministry gently reminds grieving families that they are not forgotten and remain held in prayer by the parish community.

<!-- image -->

## Anniversary Cards

Using Caring Cards that are designed by the Caring Card Ministry at St. Joseph's, card are prayerfully handwritten by a volunteer and sent to the family of those who have had a Funeral Mass at either of our sister parishes on the one-year anniversary of their loved one's death. It is to remind them that they are remembered and supported in prayer by the Parish Community of St. Joseph and Holy Spirit.
